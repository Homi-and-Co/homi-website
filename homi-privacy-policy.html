Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Homi & Co. ("we," "us") provides AI business orchestration services for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to hello@homiand.co.

Information we collect

  • Contact and business details you share. When you fill out a form on our site, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, business name, operating stage, and descriptions of your workflows and recurring friction. This helps us understand whether our services fit your business.
  • Communications. When you email us or book a conversation, we keep a record so we can respond and follow up.
  • Website analytics. We use the Meta Pixel and standard analytics to understand how visitors use the site, which pages are helpful, and how people find us. This collects device and browsing information such as pages visited, approximate location, and referral source. You can control ad tracking through your browser and device settings and through Meta's ad preferences.

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and evaluate fit for our services.
  • To deliver the services you purchase, including the Business Blueprint, Build, and Managed Orchestration.
  • To improve our website, messaging, and services.
  • To send occasional updates about Homi & Co. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.

We do not sell your personal information.

How information is stored and shared

  • Form submissions are processed through Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets under our account.
  • We share information only with the service providers we need to operate (for example, form processing, email, and analytics), and only for the purposes described here.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, our clients, or others.

Client work stays separate

When you become a client, your business data, accounts, and client-specific configuration remain yours. Homi & Co. retains only its reusable methods, frameworks, templates, and governance system. We never mix one client's data into another client's work.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as it is useful for evaluating fit and maintaining a business relationship, and client records for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time by writing to hello@homiand.co.

Your choices and rights

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Disable cookies and ad tracking in your browser or device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will honor all applicable requests.

Children

Our services are for businesses, not children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. The date at the top shows the latest revision, and material changes will be noted here.
