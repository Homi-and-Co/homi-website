07 / FAQ

Straight answers before we start.

What does Homi & Co. do?
We help small businesses organize routine work, connect the tools they use, and keep follow-up moving. People stay in charge of important decisions.
How is this different from hiring an AI agency or consultant?
We begin with the way your business actually works, then recommend changes that fit. We can also help set them up and maintain them.
What does the Blueprint cost?
Founding member pricing is available for a limited time. Request a Blueprint and we will confirm current pricing with you.
What do I need to have in place before we start?
You need an operating business, access to the tools involved, and someone who can explain how your team works and approve key decisions. We will discuss any additional tools before you commit.
Do you replace my staff?
No. We remove or automate routine work, support the work that benefits from AI, and keep people responsible for judgment, trust, and relationships.
Who owns my data and accounts?
You do. Your accounts, data, policies, and reports stay under your ownership. We work inside your systems, and nothing is held hostage.
